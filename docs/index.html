🚀 Getting Started

  1. Install the App – Get Quick Test Management from the Atlassian Marketplace and install it on your Jira Cloud site.
  2. Open Any Issue – Navigate to any Jira issue where you want to track test status.
  3. Find the Panel – Look for "Test Status" in the issue's right-hand details area.
  4. Set Status – Click on a status button (Pass, Fail, Blocked, In Progress) to update the test state.
  5. Add Notes – Optionally add test notes. They auto-save as you type.
  6. View History – Expand the history section to see the last 10 test runs.
  7. Search by Result – In Jira search, use testStatus = failed, testStatus in (failed, blocked) or testStatusUpdated >= -7d.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored in Atlassian Forge Storage, and the current result is also saved as a Jira issue property so it can be searched with JQL. We do not use any external servers or databases. Your data never leaves Atlassian.

Can the developer access my data?

No. Forge's architecture ensures that app developers cannot access customer data. We have no ability to view, retrieve, or access any data stored by this App in your Jira instance.

Is this app GDPR compliant?

Yes. Because we use Atlassian Forge Storage, your data benefits from Atlassian's enterprise-grade compliance, including GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2, and ISO 27001.

How do I delete my data?

Use "Reset" in the App to clear the notes and set an issue back to untested. Uninstalling the App removes app storage according to Atlassian's data retention policies.

How much does it cost?

It's free for teams of up to 10 users. Larger teams pay a small per-user monthly price, shown on the Marketplace listing, with a free trial.
