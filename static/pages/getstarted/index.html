Get started with Quick Test Management

Record Pass, Fail, Blocked or In progress on any Jira issue, with notes and a history of recent runs. No setup is needed; the app works as soon as it's installed.

For your team

  1. Open any issue and find the Test Status panel in the issue's right-hand details area.
  2. Click Pass, Fail, Blocked or Testing. Add notes such as steps to reproduce; they save automatically.
  3. Open Test History in the panel to see the last 10 runs.

Find issues by test result

Every result is searchable in Jira search, filters and dashboards:

  • testStatus = failed: all failing issues
  • testStatus in (failed, blocked) AND sprint in openSprints(): what's blocking this sprint
  • testStatusUpdated >= -7d: results recorded in the last week

Values are passed, failed, blocked, in_progress and untested. Issues are searchable once a result has been recorded on them.

Permissions

  • Anyone who can view an issue can see its test results.
  • Recording or resetting results needs the Edit issues project permission, so your existing Jira permission schemes apply.

Privacy

Results and notes are stored in Atlassian's Forge storage and as a Jira issue property. The app sends no data outside Atlassian and stores no personal data.
